Welcome, explorer!

A quiet village rests in the meadow. Each cottage holds a card about a topic. Walk up to a cottage and step inside to collect its card, then revisit them anytime in your Journal.

Move: Arrow keys / WASD (or the on-screen pad)
Enter a cottage: press E (or the ENTER button)
Journal: press M
Tip: roofs are colour-coded to hint at what each cottage contains.
