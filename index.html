Welcome, explorer!

The land is divided into six regions, each shaped by a different kind of power. Every region hides a hut — and a 🔑 key. Find the region's key before its hut will open and give up its card.

Move: Arrow keys / WASD (or the pad)
Pick up a key: walk over it · Enter a hut: press E
Journal: press M
Keys float and glow above the scenery — follow the shimmer to find them.
