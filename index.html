Welcome, explorer!

The land is divided into six regions, each shaped by a different kind of power. Every region hides a hut — and a 🔑 key. You must find the region's key before its hut will open and give up its card.

Move: Arrow keys / WASD (or the pad)
Pick up a key: just walk over it
Enter a hut: press E (or ENTER)
Journal: press M
Tip: keys are small and glint in the light — explore each region's interior to spot them.
